Manage your lessons

To manage your bookings, please click the Register or Sign in button at the top right corner of the calendar panel below.

No credits yet? Purchase a package first — after payment, your account is credited automatically. See lesson packages.
How it works

Once logged in, you can:

  • Simply book a slot and your credits are deducted automatically. Each lesson costs 1 credit.
  • View all your upcoming and past lessons.
  • Change a booking by deleting your current reservation and selecting a new available time slot (at least 24 hours in advance).
  • Check your remaining lesson balance in your account settings.
